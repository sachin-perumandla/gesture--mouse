Gesture guide

Try these movements

Detection is approximate and works best with good lighting and your full hand visible.

☝️

Index finger up

Shows “Move pointer” when only the index finger is raised.

✌️

Two fingers up

Shows “Scroll” when index and middle fingers are raised.

✊

Closed fist

Shows “Pause / resume” as a detected gesture label.

🤏

Thumb + index pinch

Shows “Click” when the thumb and index fingertips get close.

🖐️

Open hand

Shows “Open hand” when multiple fingers are raised.
